Harmonised Events

__EVENTS_TABLE__

A pair is a TEST_NAME + PREPROCESED_UNIT. Status is the mapping_status of its mapping; NOT-FOUND is a pair that is not in the harmonization table. Pairs with a warning have a message of a warning table (ignored or not).

Harmonised Values

__VALUES_TABLE__

The harmonised deciles of a pair are compared with a reference: the median, decile by decile, of the other approved pairs in the same LOINC Group (the pair is never part of its own reference). KS is the Kolmogorov-Smirnov distance between the two sets of deciles, from 0 (same) to 0.9 (no overlap).

Click a header to sort. Filters: text matches anywhere (case insensitive); numeric columns take >0.5, <=2, =0.3 or a range 0.2..0.5. The bars are the share of records by source: blue Source, orange Extracted (value) or PrimaryInjection (unit), red QCOut or SecondaryCorrection, grey missing.
