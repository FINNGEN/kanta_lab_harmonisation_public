Fixed Units

__FIXED_TABLE__

The fixes are the rows of unitFix.tsv. A unit is fixed when the PREPROCESED_UNIT of a mapped pair is replaced by a different UNIT by unitFix.tsv (the Unit change column of the Table tab). The percentage is of all the records.

Harmonised test+unit

__EVENTS_TABLE__

A pair is a TEST_NAME + PREPROCESED_UNIT. Status is the mapping_status of its mapping; MISSING is a pair that is not in the harmonisation table, and ERROR counts every ERROR:<reason>. Warnings are the messages of the two warning tables of step 4 (a pair has at most one of each): the number not marked ignore, and in brackets the total.

Harmonised Values

__VALUES_TABLE__

The harmonised deciles of a pair are compared with a reference: the median, decile by decile, of the other approved pairs in the same LOINC Group, or of the other approved pairs with the same concept when the concept is in no LOINC Group (the pair is never part of its own reference). KS is the Kolmogorov-Smirnov distance between the two sets of deciles, from 0 (same) to 0.9 (no overlap).

Click a header to sort. Filters: text matches anywhere (case insensitive); numeric columns take >0.5, <=2, =0.3 or a range 0.2..0.5. The bars are the share of records by source: blue Source, orange Extracted (value) or PrimaryInjection (unit), red QCOut or SecondaryCorrection, grey missing; hover a bar for the percentages and the (approximate) number of records. Under the harmonised deciles are the deciles of the reference, followed by the number of pairs they come from, e.g. (36). The reference of a pair is one of the LOINC Groups of its concept (the first with enough approved pairs); In reference = yes marks the pairs that make up the references. The harmonised unit is shown only when it is not the unit of the test.
